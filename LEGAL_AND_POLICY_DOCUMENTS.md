# Exceptional People Mingle (EPM) — Legal, Compliance & Policy Documents

To launch and operate **Exceptional People Mingle (EPM)** legally and securely, the following documentation and policies are tailored specifically for the platform.

---

## 1. Terms & Conditions (T&C)

- **User Accounts & Eligibility**: Rules governing account creation, identity verification, age requirements, and profile accuracy for all community members.
- **Platform Role**: Explicit statement that EPM acts as a platform facilitating connections and networking between individuals, outlining liability limits.
- **Conduct & Acceptable Use**: Rules against spam, harassment, hateful content, abusive behavior, and fraudulent postings or profile creation.

---

## 2. Privacy Policy

- **Data Collection & Usage**: Disclosure of personal data collected (name, email, profile data, location, media uploads, and device metadata).
- **Third-Party Services**: Transparent disclosure of integrated services including Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Storage) and AI services.
- **Data Retention & Deletion**: Clear instructions for users on how to request profile deletion, account termination, and data export in compliance with global privacy regulations (GDPR / CCPA).

---

## 3. Terms of Service (ToS)

- **Service Agreements**: Binding agreement terms between platform users upon connecting and interacting within the application.
- **Community Standards & Conduct**: Guidelines on acceptable platform usage, interaction etiquette, and grounds for temporary or permanent account suspension.

---

## 4. Copyright & Content Policy (DMCA / Fair Use)

- **User-Generated Content**: Rights and ownership guidelines regarding photos, text, and media uploaded by users to their profiles.
- **Takedown Request Mechanism**: A clear, dedicated process for copyright holders to request the removal of unauthorized or infringing content posted on EPM.

---

## 5. About Us & Company Overview Document

- **Store Submission & Compliance**: Official company biography, vision, and mission statement required for Apple App Store and Google Play Store developer review and publication.
- **User & Partner Transparency**: Informational content explaining the origin of Exceptional People Mingle (EPM), core values, community guidelines, and service offerings to users.
